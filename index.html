Privacy Policy — Korean Hangul Basic, Korean Hangul Advanced, Korean Hangul Words, Korean Hangul All-in-One (Android · iOS)

Effective date: 2026-10-31 · Operator: HiClone (주식회사 하이클론) · Contact: info@hiclone.net

This policy applies to the Android versions of the four apps above, distributed on Google Play (package names com.hiclone.hangulzzang_app.basic, .middle, .advanced and .full). Sections 1 to 6 describe the Android versions. Section 7 describes how the iOS versions distributed on the App Store differ. If we release the apps on another platform, we will update this policy before that release.

The apps are free to download. Each app offers one one-time in-app purchase that unlocks that app's full course. The apps do not require an account, show no advertising and contain no advertising identifier or analytics tool of our own.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress and settings, and the record of a verified purchase (product, a transaction reference and the time of verification), are stored on your device. We do not sync your learning progress or settings to HiClone's servers. Section 2 describes the purchase record our server keeps.

Depending on your Android settings and the backup or device-transfer method you use, Android may make a copy of this data. Uninstalling the app deletes the app's data on that device. It does not delete copies made elsewhere.

2. Purchase verification (our server)

What the app sends. Only when you buy or restore the full course, the app sends three items to our verification server over HTTPS: the platform name, the product ID and the purchase token issued by Google Play. It sends nothing else, and it does not ask for your name, email address or any account.

What the server does. The server asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid and returns the result to the app. To do this it receives from Google Play the purchase status, the product ID, the order ID, the purchase time and the country or region of the purchase. It does not store the purchase token.

Purchase record. For each verified purchase the server keeps one record in Google Cloud Firestore (Seoul region): the platform, the product ID, the order ID, the country or region of the purchase, the purchase time, when and how many times the purchase was verified, and whether it was later refunded or canceled. Google Play also notifies our server when a purchase is completed, canceled or refunded, and we update the record. The record contains no name, email address, payment details or purchase token. We use it to confirm purchases, to handle refunds and purchase inquiries, to prevent fraudulent purchase claims and to count sales. Only authorized HiClone staff can view it, through an internal management page.

What is logged. The server runs on Google Cloud Run (Seoul region), which processes requests for us as our hosting provider. Request logs record the IP address, device user agent, requested address, time and response status. The server also logs the product ID, the verification result and the first 8 characters of the order ID. We use these logs only to operate the service, to fix errors and to prevent fraudulent purchase claims. We do not use them to identify or track you.

How long. Under our current settings request logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. Purchase records are kept for 5 years from the purchase, or from the refund or cancellation if that is later, and are then deleted automatically. If we change these periods, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

3. Other providers that receive information

  • Google Play (payments and purchase checks). Google Play processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Cloudflare (internal management page). The management page through which our staff view the purchase records in section 2 is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes those records for us when staff open the page.
  • Google Play Billing Library (included in the app). To connect to Google Play's payment system, the app includes Google's billing library. When the app starts, this library sends technical diagnostic information to Google. In our own testing this included the library and app version, package name, Android version, device manufacturer and model, language and region settings, network type, mobile carrier code, time zone and the result code of the billing connection. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it.
  • Speech recognition service (optional). Read-aloud practice is optional. It asks for microphone permission and uses the speech recognition service selected on your device, but only when that service is provided by Google (the Google app or Speech Services by Google). If another service is selected or none is available, the app does not offer the microphone check; you can still read aloud and compare with the answer video. Google may process your voice on its servers to recognize it, under the Google Privacy Policy. According to the data safety information Google publishes for these services on Google Play, they encrypt data in transit. The app itself does not receive or store audio. It receives only the recognized text, uses it immediately to check your answer and does not store or send it. The app shows this notice before it first asks for microphone permission. You can refuse or withdraw microphone permission at any time in system settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose information to anyone other than the providers described above, except where required by law.

4. Children

The apps are intended for users aged 13 and over. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us at info@hiclone.net and we will delete the information we hold.

5. Your choices and contact

You can use the free lessons without buying anything and without microphone permission. For questions about this policy or about the server logs and purchase records described in section 2, or to ask us to show or delete your purchase record, contact info@hiclone.net with your order ID (on iOS, your transaction ID). We may keep a record where the law requires it.

6. Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. This includes any change to the retention periods in section 2 and any new provider.

7. iOS versions (App Store)

This policy also applies to the iOS versions of the four apps, distributed on the App Store (bundle IDs com.hiclone.hangulzzang.basic, .complete, .advanced and com.hiclone.hangulzzang). Sections 1 to 6 apply to them, with the following differences.

  • Information stored on your device. Depending on your iCloud or computer backup settings, iOS may include the app's data in your backups.
  • Purchase verification. Only when you buy or restore the full course, the app sends the platform name, the product ID and the signed transaction issued by the App Store to our verification server over HTTPS. The server checks Apple's signature, the app and the product, and whether the purchase was refunded, and returns the result. It does not store the signed transaction. It keeps the purchase record described in section 2, with the original transaction ID in place of the order ID and the App Store country or region, and the App Store notifies our server of purchases, refunds and revocations. It logs the product ID, the verification result and the first 8 characters of the transaction ID, together with the request logs described in section 2. If our server cannot be reached, the app checks Apple's signature on the device instead.
  • Payments. Apple processes your payment under its own terms and the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment card details. The iOS versions do not include the Google Play Billing Library.
  • Speech recognition (optional). When you tap the microphone, the app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission and uses Apple's speech recognition. Apple may process your voice on its servers to recognize it, under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app receives only the recognized text, uses it immediately to check your answer and does not store or send it. You can refuse or withdraw these permissions at any time in Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Voice guidance. Spoken guidance in guided mode is generated on your device by iOS.
